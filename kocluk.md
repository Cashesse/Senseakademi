## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite, structured, and goal-oriented educational experience for modern learners, candidates, and mentors. Rooted in disciplined clarity and academic confidence, the visual aesthetic merges **Corporate / Modern** precision with high-focus dashboard ergonomics.

The atmosphere evokes focus, momentum, and professional credibility. It draws visual discipline from the corporate deep navy core while leveraging energetic crimson focal accents derived from the brand identity to drive attention, action, and motivation.

Key visual pillars:
- **Clean Architectural Blocks:** Spacious, neatly partitioned cards, soft gray containment surfaces, and low-cognitive-load layouts.
- **Deep Navy Anchor:** Solid grounding in dark navy tones for major interactive blocks, primary calls to action, active navigation tabs, and impactful metric counters.
- **Precision Accents:** Deliberate, high-impact placements of vibrant red for crucial milestones, notifications, and key conversion paths without causing visual fatigue.
- **Lightweight Linearity:** Fine structural dividers and monoline icon sets that preserve airiness and analytical order.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, high-clarity environment optimized for sustained study sessions, performance dashboards, and curriculum browsing.

### Palette Roles & Balance

- **Deep Navy (`#171F3B` - Primary):** Serves as the core visual anchor. Applied to primary CTAs, major navigation rails/active pills, top-level headings, dark countdown banners, and high-impact numerical highlights.
- **Brand Crimson (`#D92732` - Secondary):** Dedicated to high-priority alerts, urgent deadlines, live session tags, and secondary motivational micro-interactions. Used sparingly to preserve urgency and contrast.
- **Focus Cobalt (`#3062D4` - Tertiary):** Secondary accent pulled from geometric sub-elements, useful for analytical progress tracks, informative badges, and links.
- **Canvas Light Gray (`#F6F7F8`):** The master ambient backdrop, softening contrast against pure white cards to eliminate eye strain.
- **Sub-surface Fill (`#F0F2F5`):** Reserved for nested containers, timer readouts, disabled states, and table headers.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity foreground panels, popovers, and elevated modal overlays.
- **Typography:** Primary text is set in deep slate navy (`#20263A`) for superior readability without harshness; secondary metadata, timestamps, and descriptive subtitles reside in balanced slate (`#737B8C`).
- **Dividers & Outlines (`#E7E9EE`):** Uniform 1px hairline framing across cards, inputs, and tab segments.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered using **Inter** to ensure maximum legibility at varying scales, from large countdown clocks to dense curriculum lists and analytic charts.

### Typographic Principles

- **Numeric & Metric Dominance:** Data indicators (such as examination countdowns, active study timers, and score metrics) use tabular figures and bold weights (`700`–`800`) with tighter letter tracking for authoritative presence.
- **Hierarchy Division:** Section headings employ subtle negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain tightness, while supporting text retains open line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) for reading comfort.
- **Caps & Micro-Labels:** Status categories, day abbreviations, and card category tags leverage `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to establish visual separation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column modular grid with generous whitespace, mirroring the breathable structure of an executive coaching dashboard.

### Grid & Breakpoints

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, centered with `2.5rem` outer margin and `1.5rem` gutters. Persistent left-hand vertical navigation sidebar (`240px`–`260px`) paired with a dynamic two- or three-column content workspace.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margin and `1.25rem` gutters. Navigation contracts to a collapsed icon bar or slim header banner.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margin and `0.75rem` gutters. Cards stack vertically, metrics adopt horizontal scroll carousels or 2-column micro-grids, and study timer controls fix to sticky bottom sheets.

### Spacing Density Rules

- Internal card padding standardizes to `1.25rem` (`20px`) for compact operational modules and `1.5rem` (`24px`) for primary program panels.
- Content groupings inside modules conform strictly to the 4px/8px incremental scale (`space-sm` for badge-to-title offsets, `space-lg` for section headers, `space-2xl` for module separation).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface layering and soft, ambient diffusion rather than deep, heavy shadows. The look feels flat, clean, and tactfully layered.

### Elevation Tiers

- **Level 0 (Base Floor):** `#F6F7F8` page background. Completely flat with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a crisp 1px structural border (`#E7E9EE`) and a minimal, diffused shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(23, 31, 59, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(23, 31, 59, 0.02);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Elevated Modules):** Used when hovering cards, tooltips, and floating widgets:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(23, 31, 59, 0.07), 0 1px 3px rgba(23, 31, 59, 0.03);`
  - Subtle `transform: translateY(-2px);` transition.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Centered popups, coaching session pickers, and mobile navigation overlays:
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px rgba(23, 31, 59, 0.12), 0 2px 6px rgba(23, 31, 59, 0.04);`

### Border Guidance

All primary panels and inputs must incorporate the 1px hairline border in `#E7E9EE`. Dark banner blocks (`#171F3B`) do not use borders, letting their deep tone delineate boundaries naturally.

## Shapes

The design system employs a consistent `10px`–`14px` border radius across interactive surfaces, balancing professional geometry with approachable softness.

### Corner Radii Specification

- **Small Interactive Units (10px / `0.625rem`):** Buttons, form inputs, date picker tiles, and calendar day chips.
- **Standard Cards & Banners (12px–14px / `0.75rem`–`0.875rem`):** Metric tiles, study countdown banners, and nested modules.
- **Main Structural Panels (16px / `1rem`):** Full dashboard feature sections and multi-step modal frames.
- **Pill / Circular (`9999px`):** Profile avatars, status indicators, and notification count badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#171F3B`) background, pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), `10px` border radius, `0 10px 20px -5px rgba(23, 31, 59, 0.25)` active shadow. Hover transitions to `#202B4F`.
- **Accent / Urgency CTA:** Brand Crimson (`#D92732`) background with white text, utilized for live webinar joins, trial starts, or priority tasks. Hover shifts to `#BF1E29`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure white background, 1px `#E7E9EE` border, `#20263A` label. Hover activates `#F0F2F5` tint with border darkening to `#D3D6DE`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#737B8C` icon and text; transitions to `#171F3B` on hover.

### Cards & Banners
- **White Data Card:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E7E9EE`, radius `14px`, ambient shadow.
- **Navy Feature Banner (e.g., Exam Countdown):** Solid `#171F3B` fill, white bold numerals, muted slate secondary text (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`), radius `14px`. May include delicate dot-grid watermarks for depth.
- **Metric Micro-Card:** Subtle tinted background (e.g., `#FFF5EB` for streaks, `#EFF6FF` for hours), soft border, radius `12px`, centered metric and micro-label.

### Navigation Items & Sidebar
- **Inactive Link:** Transparent fill, `#737B8C` typography, 20px monoline icon. Radius `10px`.
- **Active Link:** High-contrast Deep Navy (`#171F3B`) background, pure white text and icon, chevron indicator on the trailing edge.

### Chips & Calendar Tiles
- **Date Selector Chips:** Vertical pill format with weekday label (`label-sm`, `#737B8C`) stacked over day number (`title-md`). Inactive states use `#FFFFFF` or `#F0F2F5`; active selection is `#171F3B` with inverted white text.
- **Status Badges:** `label-sm` semibold, `6px` vertical and `10px` horizontal padding, `9999px` radius. Neutral (`#F0F2F5` / `#737B8C`), Success (`#E8F7EE` / `#168B44`), Live/Urgent (`#FDECEE` / `#D92732`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E7E9EE`, radius `10px`, text color `#20263A`. Placeholder in `#737B8C`. Focus ring: 2px Deep Navy (`#171F3B`) offset by 1px white margin.
- **Timer / Counter Display:** Elevated container using `#F6F7F8` or `#FFFFFF`, featuring heavy tabular monospace-aligned digits (`48px`), flanked by full-width Navy `#171F3B` Start/Pause buttons.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px box with 1.5px border (`#D3D6DE`), transitions to solid `#171F3B` when checked, with crisp white checkmark glyph.

### Empty States & Instructional Blocks
- Uses linear vector iconography enclosed in `#F0F2F5` circles, centered vertically with `14px` medium secondary body text (`#737B8C`).
